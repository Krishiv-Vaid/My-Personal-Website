Selected work

Hardware · Aerospace

5,000-foot Instrumented Rocket

Designed and built my own rocket that reached approximately 5,000 feet, featuring a custom airframe, recovery system, and compact avionics bay. The avionics system used an Arduino Nano (ATmega328P), BMP388 barometric pressure sensor, and MicroSD storage to record timestamped flight data at 50 Hz through custom C++ firmware. The rocket also included static pressure ports, an onboard FPV camera, and post-flight sensor and video analysis.
